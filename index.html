How It Works

A private real estate lending business operating in Bali, Indonesia

We offer long-term mortgage-style loans and construction debt to buyers and owners of properties held by foreign entities (Dubai, USA, Singapore, EU). Loans are secured by a triple-layer security stack: registered Hak Tanggungan over HGB-titled property, a share pledge over the Indonesian PT PMA holding company, and a personal guarantee from the borrower.

Why Foreign-Entity Owned Properties

Our focus on foreign-entity ownership solves the biggest structural risks in Bali private lending

Clean HGB Title

Foreign entities cannot legally hold Bali property through nominee structures — they must use PT PMA with HGB. This title class is fully mortgageable via Hak Tanggungan and enforceable through BPN.

Sophisticated Counterparty

An entity backed by UAE, US, or Singapore principals understands contract law, has reputational skin in the game across jurisdictions, and is more likely to service debt and cooperate on workouts.

USD-Aligned

Foreign entities price their Bali assets in USD, market to international buyers, and receive rental income in USD. Denominating our loan in USD removes the FX mismatch that affects IDR-denominated loans.

Triple-Layer Security Architecture

Every loan is structured around three independent security layers

1

Hak Tanggungan

Registered Indonesian Mortgage

A statutory mortgage registered with the National Land Agency (BPN) against the HGB certificate. Grants the lender the right to enforce against the land and structures through public auction or private sale.

2

Share Pledge — PT PMA

Indonesian Holding Entity

A pledge over the shares of the Indonesian PT PMA that holds the HGB title. In a default event, this allows the lender to take control of the entity that owns the property.

3

Personal Guarantee

Home-Jurisdiction Enforcement

An unlimited personal guarantee from the beneficial owner(s), governed by the law of their home jurisdiction (UAE, US, Singapore, UK). Makes the individual personally liable for the debt.

Process

From inquiry to disbursement

1

Inquiry & Qualification

Submit a loan inquiry. We conduct preliminary qualification checks against HGB title and foreign-entity ownership requirements.

2

Term Sheet

Issue a formal term sheet under the PT PMA, outlining loan structure, pricing, and security package.

3

Documentation

Full due diligence via BPN title search, MAPPI valuation, legal opinion. Execute Hak Tanggungan, share pledge, and personal guarantee through PPAT.

4

Disbursement

Register Hak Tanggungan before drawdown. Disburse loan in USD upon satisfaction of conditions precedent.
